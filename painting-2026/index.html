§ 02 — Positioning

Why a contractor, not a painter.

/ 01

Proper surface prep.

We treat walls the way we treat structures. Crack repair, render inspection, moisture testing, and waterproofing integration happen before paint goes on — not as expensive rework later.

/ 02

Single accountability.

One licensed contractor, one project manager, one warranty. No subcontracting chaos. If the paint fails and the underlying issue is render or waterproofing, you call us and we fix it.

/ 03

Municipality compliant.

NOC handling, developer permissions, HSE documentation, and insurance are standard. Work on occupied commercial buildings or villa compounds requires paperwork most painters don't carry.

The materials we specify.

Brand selection isn't decorative — it's matched to substrate, exposure, and use. Final spec. is confirmed in your scope document before any paint is opened.
