§ 02 — Position

Why a contractor,
not a fabricator.

/ 01

Foundations done right.

A pergola is only as good as what it's bolted to. We pour proper footings, core-drill to structural walls, and engineer for UAE wind loads — not just bolt to paving and hope.

/ 02

DM approvals included.

Dubai Municipality requires approved drawings for permanent outdoor structures. We handle the full submission — clients don't chase approvals or engage a separate consultant.

/ 03

One team, one warranty.

Design, fabrication, installation, and warranty — all from Al Hadeeqa's own licensed crew. If something needs attention in year three, you call the same number you called to order it.

§ 03 — Process

From visit to handover.

Four steps. Roughly four to six weeks for the most popular tier — most of which is fabrication, not waiting on us.

  1. 01
    Day 1 · Free site visit

    We visit, measure, and listen.

    We come to your property, take measurements, review the space, and discuss the brief and budget. No charge, no pressure.

  2. 02
    Week 1 · 3D design + DM

    Drawings, then approvals.

    Design drawings prepared and approved with you, then submitted to Dubai Municipality on your behalf.

  3. 03
    Week 2–4 · Fabrication

    Cut, weld, powder-coat.

    Aluminium cut, welded and powder-coated in-house. Louvered and motorised systems are assembled and tested before they leave.

  4. 04
    2–4 days on site

    Install & handover.

    Foundations, frame, roof, lighting, drainage, controls demo, and a written warranty in your hand.
